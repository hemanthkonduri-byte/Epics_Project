Privacy Policy for AI Knowledge System

Last updated: November 2, 2026

AI Knowledge System ("we", "our", or "the app") provides an intelligent educational workspace for lecture analysis, note generation, real-time meetings, and AI-powered study assistance. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your name, educational or personal email address, and encrypted credentials to manage your account and send 6-digit verification codes.
  • Audio & Video Data: When you upload lecture recordings or join a live meeting, audio and video streams are processed exclusively to provide transcription, lecture summaries, key concepts, quizzes, and live communication.
  • AI Assistant Queries: Questions and study prompts submitted to Askora and Ask AI are used only to generate real-time answers and study material.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required during live interactive classes and meetings to transmit your voice to instructors and fellow students.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Required during live video sessions so you can share your video feed with meeting participants.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Required to connect to the cloud AI service, retrieve transcripts, and authenticate your account.

3. How We Use and Protect Your Data

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or lecture recordings to third parties or advertisers.
  • All network communication is conducted using secure HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Account passwords are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard scrypt algorithms.
  • Saved recordings in ClassVault are retained for educational revision and can be deleted by you at any time.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver advanced educational intelligence, the app integrates with trusted enterprise AI providers (OpenAI, Google Gemini) via encrypted server-side APIs strictly for prompt execution and content summarization. These providers do not use your private educational submissions to train public models.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may delete any saved class or study material directly inside ClassVault. If you wish to delete your entire account and associated data, please contact us at hemanthkonduri32@gmail.com.
