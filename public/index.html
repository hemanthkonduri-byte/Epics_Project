      Help & Guide

      How is my email verified?

      After sign-up a 6-digit code is emailed to you. Only someone who can open that inbox can enter it, so it proves the address exists and is yours. College emails work the same way. Temporary-mail domains are blocked.

      What does the Analyzer give me?

      A speaker-wise transcript (Name: what they said), summary, key points, important insights, tasks with deadlines, a 15-question quiz (Easy, Medium, Hard) and flip flashcards.

      How do task alerts work?

      When a teacher says “submit the diagram by Friday”, it is listed under Tasks. Press Email alert (or Send all alerts) to get it in your inbox.

      Host vs Participant?

      Hosts can start rooms, mute everyone, lock the room, record and end the meeting. Participants join with a code, raise a hand and leave.

      What is the Ask AI button?

      After analysing a video, ask a question and it points to who said it and when.

      Staying safe in meetings

      Share the code only with your class, lock the room once everyone has joined, and never share your password.
