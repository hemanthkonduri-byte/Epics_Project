      Help & Guide

      Help and common questions are now part of your Account section.

      How is my email verified?

      After sign-up, a 6-digit code is emailed to you. College emails work too.

      What does the Analyzer provide?

      A transcript, summary, key points, insights, tasks, quiz and flashcards based on your recording.

      Host vs Participant?

      Hosts can create and manage meeting rooms. Participants join using a meeting code or invitation link.

      How do I use Askora?

      Open the separate Askora section, type a question, or tap a suggested prompt. Askora can answer general questions and help with learning.

      Staying safe

      Never share your password or private API keys. Share meeting links only with intended participants.
